# Governance

The Rathkit RTE benchmark repository is run by its owner, Rathkit LLC, through the maintainer listed in
[MEMBERS.md](MEMBERS.md). While the project is young the owner decides, and this page says how
that is done so it is not a surprise.

## Roles

| Role | GitHub access | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone | Read | Fork, open issues, send pull requests |
| Contributor | Triage | Also label, assign and close issues and pull requests, and help newcomers |
| Maintainer | Write | Also review and merge pull requests |
| Owner | Admin | Settings, releases, who holds each role |

Roles are held by GitHub teams in the organization, so membership is the same in every repository
of that organization. Nobody except the owner creates branches in the repositories. Maintainers
also work from forks.

## Becoming a contributor

Open an issue with the "Apply for a role" form. You need:

- A GitHub account with two-factor authentication on.
- A GPG key on your account, and verified signed commits.
- At least one merged pull request in a Rathkit repository, or a specific plan for one.
- Agreement to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), the [AI policy](AI_POLICY.md) and the
  [DCO](DCO).

The owner replies on the issue. If the answer is yes, you are invited to the organization's
contributors team and added to [MEMBERS.md](MEMBERS.md). If it is no, the reply says why and what
would change it.

## Becoming a maintainer

By invitation, at the owner's discretion. Maintainers have a record of good changes and of reviews
that help others, and they follow the rules on this page without being reminded. Anyone can
suggest a contributor for the role by opening an issue.

## Leaving a role

You can step down at any time by telling the owner. A role may be removed after about a year of
no activity, or immediately for a Code of Conduct violation. Removal is noted on the issue.

## Decisions

Maintainers decide on pull requests. The owner decides anything else, including direction,
releases, licensing and the roles on this page. Decisions that affect users are written down in an
issue or in the changelog.

## Changing this page

Through a pull request like any other change. The owner approves it.
